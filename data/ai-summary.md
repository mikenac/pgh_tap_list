In this week's beer updates, several breweries have made notable changes to their lineups. Abjuration has introduced three new offerings, including the Candied Sour [Lemonade Slushie/Twizzlers] and a Honey Kölsch in collaboration with Deez Beez, while removing their American Style Bitter. Acclamation added "River Life" to their collection but said goodbye to the Strawberry Shortcake Sour. Dancing Gnome made a significant shift in their selections, adding three new beers, including Neo Retro IPA, while removing several others. Grist House also expanded their range with six new additions, including Command House Lager, removing three previous selections. Lastly, Lolev introduced the "White Tiger," enhancing their offerings even further.
