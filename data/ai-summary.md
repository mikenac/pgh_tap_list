In this week's beer report, several breweries have introduced exciting new offerings. Abjuration has debuted two new beers: the Hazy Pale Ale in collaboration with TAG, featuring Citra and Simcoe hops, and a Pineapple Ice Cream Cake Sour. Acclamation adds a trio of brews, including Hoppy Little Beer and Steel City Summer, while removing Old Thunder Sol X from their lineup. Dancing Gnome has also entered the scene with their Dippy and Tessellate IPAs. Additionally, Eleventh Hour introduced Dream Cannon: No 5 Summer Punch and Every Time, while bidding farewell to Happy Valley Jack and a non-alcoholic Kölsch. Lastly, Grist House has unveiled the Straight Up Porter, bringing a rich style to the craft beer community.
