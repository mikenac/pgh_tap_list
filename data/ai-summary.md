This week, the brewing scene saw a notable addition from Grist House with the introduction of five new beers: Command House Lager, Dark Night, Fire On the Hill, Kaboom Candy: B + P, and Mr. Toasty. Meanwhile, Dancing Gnome has removed the popular beer "Goblin Folk" from their lineup. Other breweries, including Abjuration, Four Points, Golden Age, Hitchhiker, and Old Thunder, have maintained their current offerings without any changes. As we move through the week, beer enthusiasts will be excited to explore Grist House's latest creations while noting the absence of "Goblin Folk."
