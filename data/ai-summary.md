This week in the beer scene, Dancing Gnome has introduced a new brew, a Czech 12° Pale Lager, adding diversity to their offerings. Meanwhile, Lolev has made some changes by adding a new beer called Parabellum while removing three others: Glyphs III, Lupula, and Mon. The other breweries, including Abjuration, Acclamation, Eleventh Hour, Four Points, Golden Age, Grist House, Hitchhiker, and Old Thunder, have maintained their current lineups without any new additions or removals. Overall, it's a week of subtle shifts in the local brewing landscape!
